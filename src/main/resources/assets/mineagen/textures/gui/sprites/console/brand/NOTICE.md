# Provider and boat marks

These sprites show which service a sign-in button or agent row belongs to. They are scaled copies of each owner's own published artwork, with nothing added or changed except size and, for boat, rounded corners on its app icon.

| Sprites | Mark | Owner | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `claude_*` | Claude spark (Clay, #D97757) | Anthropic | Anthropic press kit |
| `openai_*` | OpenAI Blossom (white), shown for Codex | OpenAI | `https://cdn.openai.com/brand/openai-logos.zip` |
| `boat_*` | boat app icon | the owner of boat.dev | `https://boat.dev/apple-touch-icon.png` |

No licence to these marks has been granted to this project. Each mark is a trademark of its owner, who keeps all rights to it. They are used only to identify the owner's service, not to suggest that the owner endorses or is affiliated with Mineagen. The project's own licence does not cover them. To ask for their removal, open an issue on this repository.

Files are named `<mark>_h<height>_s<set>.png`: `height` is the drawn height in GUI pixels and `set` the GUI scale the sprite is baked for (sprites are `height × set` pixels square).
