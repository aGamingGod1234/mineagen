# #59 Keep the shield on the skeleton after a block and meet grazing arrows where they land

- **Problem:** The bot's shield stopped only about 82% of skeleton arrow damage, even though it now rose as the skeleton drew. Tracing every tick showed why. When the shield blocked an arrow, the arrow bounced back past the bot's head, and the bot read the bounce as a new shot. It spun its shield to face the bounce, directly away from the skeleton, and never saw the next draw coming (61% of the unblocked hits). Separately, arrows that grazed the bot's side counted as side hits, because the game judges a block by where the arrow touches you, not where it was shot from (29%).
- **Solution:** Arrows that have already reached the body are ignored. While an arrow flies, the shield turns to the exact point where the game's own physics says it will touch the body. Between shots it turns back to face the archer.
- **Result:** Arrow damage blocked rose from 82.0% to 97.1% (HP taken 938 to 77 over the same number of cases, down 83%), at 95.8% or better at every range from 4 to 15 blocks. Measured live in 24 repeats per range on boat machines with vanilla's shield and damage counters, against 48 repeats of old main.
