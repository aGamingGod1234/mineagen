# #59 Keep the shield on the skeleton after a block and meet grazing arrows where they land

- **Problem:** The bot's shield stopped only about 81% of skeleton arrow damage, even though it now rose as the skeleton drew, and it did worse the farther away the skeleton was. Tracing every tick showed why. When the shield blocked an arrow, the arrow bounced back past the bot's head, and the bot read the bounce as a new shot. It spun its shield to face the bounce, directly away from the skeleton, and never saw the next draw coming (71% of the unblocked hits). Separately, arrows that grazed the bot's side counted as side hits, because the game judges a block by where the arrow touches you, not where it was shot from (28%). Skeleton aim spreads with distance, so grazing hits grow with range.
- **Solution:** The bot remembers every arrow that has already hit it or its shield and ignores it. While an arrow flies, the shield turns to the exact point where the game's own physics says it will touch the body. Between shots it turns back to face the archer.
- **Result:** In the same rooms, arrow damage blocked rose from 81.1% to 99.6%: 488 HP taken became 10 HP, 98% less. It held at 99.2% or better at every spawn distance from 4 to 15 blocks, and at 99% or better at every real shooting distance (old main fell from 100% under 5 blocks to 77-80% beyond 8). Measured live in 96 paired cases on boat machines with vanilla's shield and damage counters. The fix was better in 77 pairs, equal in 17 and worse in 2.
