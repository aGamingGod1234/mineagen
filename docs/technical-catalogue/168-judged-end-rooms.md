# 168: Judge the dragon fight and End survival in practice rooms, with and without a bow

**Problem:** nothing measured whether the bot can beat the dragon. Every End practice room checked one mechanic and had no pass bar, while recorded games that reached the End died there 16 times in 12 games.

**Solution:** a practice room on the real vanilla End. The body arrives on the arrival platform with the kit it carries in real games, a scripted player fights with whatever it holds, and the bot's own reflexes keep it alive. The pass bar is "dragon dead and body alive within 20 game minutes". A second room asks only "still alive after 10 minutes on the island".

**Result:** these are measured on boat machines, 10 runs per kit.
- **Dragon kills:** 0/10 with a bow and 0/10 without.
- **Crystals:** with the bow, the body broke crystals in 10/10 runs (4.1 of 10 on average). Without it, 0/10.
- **Deaths:** the body died in 8/10 bow fights and 9/10 no-bow fights, mostly to endermen. The dragon never took damage.
- **Survival:** idle bodies were alive after 10 minutes in 11/20 runs.
- **Takeaway:** surviving endermen, not the weapon, is what currently blocks the win.
