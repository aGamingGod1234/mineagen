# #186: Step across a drawn bow's line with no shield, and count being shot at as under fire

- **Problem:** without a shield, the bot stood still in front of skeletons and died in open ground almost every time: in a room with two skeletons and a zombie it died 26 of 30 times on main.
- **Solution:** on the same early warning the shield uses (a bow drawn at the body, or a loaded crossbow aimed at it), the bot side-steps across the shot's line around the release, the other way on the next draw, on checked footing. Being shot at, not only being hit, now also sends it for walls or cover.
- **Result:** in that room deaths fell from 9 of 10 to 1 of 10 and mean damage from 36.6 to 18.9 HP (measured, 10 runs a side on boat machines, Fisher p = 0.001). In the cover room damage fell from 7.8 to 4.5 HP with no deaths either side; reaching cover went 9 of 10 to 7 of 10, within noise (p = 0.58), after a first version that dodged every hit and so never went for cover (10 of 10 to 1 of 10) was fixed.
