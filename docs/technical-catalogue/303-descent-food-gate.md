# PR #303: Stock food on the surface before going down for iron, lava or obsidian

- **Problem:** The bot was told to stock food before going underground, but it treated that as a suggestion. In 6 of 7 fresh games it dug down for iron and lava with almost no food, built its Nether portal deep underground, and walked into the Nether starving, out of reach of the earlier gate that only guards portals on the surface.
- **Solution:** When the bot is on the surface with a stone pickaxe and still short of the food minimum, the coordinator turns the call that would take it underground (a dig down, an iron or obsidian obtain, a lava search) into a hunt-and-cook first. It does this at most twice per life, caps each hunt at 6 minutes, and lets go when no animal is around.
- **Result:** Measured in a 25-minute practice room (portal kit, herd nearby, no food), 4 runs per arm: bots that went underground with at least 200 food points went from 1 of 4 (gate off) to 3 of 4 (gate on). Food carried down was 284 to 337 points with the gate against 79 to 174 for the 3 bots that went down short without it. The cost is about 7 minutes of hunting before the descent (median 11.8 against 4.6 minutes). Deaths were equal, 1 against 1. Low trust at this sample size.
