# #97 Climb out of a flooded hole by placing a step

- **Problem:** Digging for iron over an underground lake, the bot ended up afloat in its own flooded hole with the rim two blocks above the water and nowhere dry to climb onto, and it just waited there.
- **Solution:** It puts a block down in the water beside itself, climbs onto it and digs its way back up to try somewhere else.
- **Result:** Got out of the hole in 10 of 10 runs, against 0 of 10 before (measured, 10 room runs). Escape's own 7 practice rooms: 35/35 pass, damage 17 to 5 HP (measured, 5 runs each).
