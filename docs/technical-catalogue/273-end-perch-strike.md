# #273 End: strike a perched dragon's head as soon as it lands

- **Problem:** the dragon leaves a perch in about 5 seconds unless the player is close and in its sight, and the model's commands arrive too late to use that window.
- **Solution:** a reflex-layer stance that walks to a safe spot in reach of the head and strikes the moment the dragon lands.
- **Result:** measured over 4 matched 45-minute End games a side: dragon kills 0 to 1, deaths 11 to 11, health lost per near landing 4.3 to 4.9.
