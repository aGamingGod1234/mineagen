# #253 Crystal room: pillar up to shoot the tallest crystal

- **Problem:** The bot could see the tallest End crystal only from so far away that its arrows fell short, and the test driver kept stepping back in to where the crystal hid behind its pillar.
- **Solution:** Stand a few blocks higher at a spot worked out from the pillar's width and height, where the crystal is both visible and in bow range.
- **Result:** Measured over 60 practice fights a side: the tallest crystal left standing 37 → 26, fights passed 8 → 17, deaths 18 → 16.
