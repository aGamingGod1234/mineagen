# #136: Step inland and keep the level before walling in from a blaze on a ledge

- **Problem:** in the Nether, the bot with no shield walls itself in when a blaze it cannot reach shoots it. On a fortress bridge's edge it stood at the lip to do that, and one fireball knocked it into the lava sea before a single block was placed. Four traced checkpoint deaths fell 31 to 54 blocks this way, every one during the first step of the wall-in, every one after its shield had broken.
- **Solution:** before standing still under fire, the reflex now asks the edge guard whether a lethal or unseen drop lies within a shove. If one does, it steps crouched to the nearest seen spot with none (a bridge's middle) as soon as the blaze is its focus, before the first fireball lands, and walls in there while holding its level so a push is walked back. With no such spot it takes natural cover or braces.
- **Result:** in a practice room with the bot on a bridge's far lip and a caged blaze, it stayed out of the lava 8 of 10 times, against 0 of 10 on main (Fisher p = 0.0007), and deaths fell from 10 to 2 (measured, 10 runs a side on boat). Thirteen guard rooms showed nothing worse (measured, 10 a side).
