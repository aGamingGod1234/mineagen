# #229 Fight on without a shield, eat in fight lulls, and back out of a blaze fight with rods in hand

- **Problem:** At a blaze spawner the bot died in about a fifth of its practice runs, and almost half of those deaths came after its third kill, with rods already in its pockets. It kept fighting a crowd until the caller's last-ditch flee line at 6 health, by which point the retreat was usually blocked. Separately, once fireballs broke its shield, every later "fight with a shield" call failed at once and left it standing still among blazes.
- **Solution:** Holding a blaze rod, a blaze fight now backs out at half health, as a player who already has the loot would. A missing shield no longer stops the fight, and the bot takes a bite when the fight goes quiet.
- **Result:** Calls that left the bot idle without a shield: 56 → 0 (measured, one batch of 60 spawner runs a side). Backing out with rods, 20 runs a room a side in the same batches: runs with a death went 17/60 → 7/60 (measured; p = 0.02), and rooms that kept a rod went 34/60 → 39/60 (within noise). Fighting on without a shield and eating in lulls changed pass rates within noise on their own (109/180 → 107/180 over three batches).
