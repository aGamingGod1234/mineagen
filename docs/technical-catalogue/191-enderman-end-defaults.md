# #191 Keep endermen off the bot in the End

- **Problem:** in the End, endermen killed most bots before the dragon fight went anywhere: 20 of 34 deaths in 40 judged End rooms. The bot never added the "look away" rule the guide asked for, and once an enderman was angry it teleported beside the bot faster than the bot could run or build.
- **Solution:** two new End instincts that arm on arrival: turn the view off a calm enderman's eyes, and pour the water bucket at your feet when an enderman comes for you, since water hurts endermen and makes them teleport away.
- **Result:** deaths in the judged End rooms fell from 34 of 40 to 25 of 40, and enderman deaths from 20 to 12 (measured on Boat, 20 fight and 20 idle-survival rooms a side, two-sided Fisher p 0.041).
